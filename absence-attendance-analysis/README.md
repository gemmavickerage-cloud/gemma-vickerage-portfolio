# Employee Absence & Attendance Analysis
# 📌 Business Problem
Northstar Distribution Group is a large UK organisation employing approximately 2,500 people across multiple departments, locations, and shift patterns. Senior leadership has identified employee absence as a growing operational concern. Absence levels vary significantly between departments, locations, and working patterns, but the organisation lacks a clear, centralised view of the data.

# 📌 Project Objective
To analyse 12 months of employee absence data, identify key trends and patterns, and provide leadership with actionable insights into the scale, causes, and distribution of absence.
The project includes:

Data quality assessment and cleansing (SQL)

Analytical modelling and transformation (Power BI)

Visualisation and dashboard development

Evidence‑based recommendations for leadership

# 🛠 Tools Used
SQL — Data cleansing and preparation

Power BI — Data modelling, transformation, and visualisation

# 📊 Data Visualisation Approach
Key Visualisation Choices
KPI Cards  
Display core metrics such as total absence days, number of absence records, average duration, absence rate, and number of employees with absence. These provide immediate, high‑level insight.

Line Chart — Absence Trend Over Time  
Shows how absence fluctuates month‑to‑month across 12 months, highlighting seasonal patterns and spikes.

Donut Chart — Absence Category Breakdown  
Illustrates the proportion of short‑term vs long‑term absence.

Pie Chart — Top 5 Absence Reasons  
Highlights the most common reasons for absence (e.g., cold/flu, mental health, musculoskeletal).

Interactive Slicers  
Allow filtering by department, location, and employment type for targeted analysis.

Top 5 Employee Table  
Ranks employees by total absence using DAX measures for dynamic ranking and contribution percentages.

Tooltip Pages  
Provide additional context when hovering over employees, including profile information and absence contribution metrics.

Outcome
The final dashboard provides a clear, interactive, and insightful view of absence trends across the organisation. Leaders can quickly identify patterns, compare departments or locations, and understand the drivers behind absence levels.

# 📌 Recommendations
Provide additional support for cold and flu cases, including flu jab provision.

Strengthen mental health support and promote the Employee Assistance Programme.

Implement clearer short‑term and long‑term absence policies.

Conduct targeted reviews in Derby and Engineering (highest absence location/department) to assess managerial support.

Manage seasonal peaks in absence through staffing adjustments in March, May, and November.

# 📈 Next Steps (Future Enhancements)
If more time or data were available, the dashboard could be expanded with:

Monthly and Seasonal Analysis
More detailed month‑by‑month breakdowns to help managers anticipate upcoming peaks and plan resources.

Year‑on‑Year Comparison
Once future years of data are available, add YoY visuals to track long‑term trends and evaluate interventions.

Enhanced Employee‑Level Insights
Incorporate demographic fields such as age groups, tenure groups, or contract types for deeper segmentation.

Policy Monitoring and Compliance
Add indicators showing when employees trigger policy thresholds and whether follow‑up actions have been completed.

Manager and Team‑Level Context
Include manager information or team hierarchy in tooltips or drill‑through pages to support targeted interventions.

Predictive or Forecasting Models
Use historical data to forecast future absence levels and identify emerging risks.

Root‑Cause Exploration
If more detailed absence reasons or HR case notes become available, perform deeper diagnostic analysis to understand underlying causes.
